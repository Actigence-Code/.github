# Code of conduct

Keep collaboration respectful, practical and welcoming. Discuss ideas and evidence, give actionable feedback, and respect other people's time and boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted personal contact and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Do not publish another person's identity, contact details or private correspondence without permission.

Raise concerns with the repository maintainers through an established private channel. Share only the information needed to assess the concern. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, limit participation or restrict access according to the circumstances.

This applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and other project collaboration spaces. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
